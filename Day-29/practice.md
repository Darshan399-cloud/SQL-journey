# Practice Tasks

## Task 1

Create an employees table with employee_id, name, department and salary.

## Task 2

Insert at least 6 employees.

## Task 3

Use CASE to categorize employees as High, Medium and Low based on salary.

## Task 4

Use Simple CASE to convert department names into department categories.

## Task 5

Create a salary status using CASE.

## Task 6

Use CASE with COUNT to count employees earning 60000 or more.

## Task 7

Use CASE with SUM to calculate the total salary of employees earning 60000 or more.

## Task 8

Use CASE with ORDER BY to create a custom sorting order.

## Task 9

Use CASE with GROUP BY to categorize departments based on average salary.
