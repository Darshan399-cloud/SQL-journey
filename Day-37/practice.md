# Practice Tasks

## Task 1

Create an employees table with employee_id, name, email, phone, manager_id and salary.

## Task 2

Insert at least 6 employees with some NULL values.

## Task 3

Find employees whose manager_id is NULL.

## Task 4

Find employees whose email is not NULL.

## Task 5

Use IFNULL() to replace missing phone numbers with 'Not Available'.

## Task 6

Use IFNULL() to replace missing emails with 'No Email'.

## Task 7

Use COALESCE() to return phone, email or 'No Contact'.

## Task 8

Use NULLIF() with two equal values.

## Task 9

Use NULLIF() with two different values.

## Task 10

Compare COUNT(*) and COUNT(email).
